# Asset Metadata

## Owner
Rotimi Alade

## What this is
A benchmark asset for an Overleaf/LaTeX research project on statistical modelling of factors affecting student academic performance.

## Project contents
The project develops a research document through five milestones:
1. Project foundation
2. Literature review and citation integration
3. Methodology and regression framework
4. Statistical model, results and validation
5. Conclusion and complete research document

## Planted defects
None.

## Decoys
The project contains one intentional decoy:
- `project_source/archived_student_variables.csv` — a plausible supplementary dataset that is not used by the research document or analysis.

## Room to vary
The benchmark can vary which project files or sections require attention while preserving the same overall research topic, modelling approach, and dataset structure.
